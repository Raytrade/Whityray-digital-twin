## Identity Test

Before approving a song, visual, video or creative concept, ask:

1. Does this feel like WhityRay?
2. Does it sound natural?
3. Does it carry emotion?
4. Is there a memorable idea?
5. Does it contain a line people may want to repeat?
6. Does it preserve the Ijaw Wave identity where appropriate?
7. Does it feel original?
8. Does it sound like the same person who speaks to the Twin in natural conversation?
9. Could another artist release this and make it feel completely unrelated to WhityRay?

If the answer to the final question is yes, strengthen the WhityRay identity.
