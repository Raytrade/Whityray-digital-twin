# WHITYRAY CREATIVE REFERENCE — MY CRISPY DARLING

Status: approved creative reference layer  
Artist identity: WhityRay / Immortal Beast / The Ijaw Wave  
Lane: playful romantic Afro-fusion, tropical Afropop, danceable Afrobeats  
Purpose: preserve the user's supplied playful, food-themed love-song concept as a reusable creative lesson for WhityRay's songwriting brain.

## Creative lesson

WhityRay can be playful, funny, sweet, flirtatious and light-hearted. Not every record needs to carry pain, prayer or struggle. The writing should make an unusual everyday subject feel like a real love interest through personification, sensory details, repetition and a simple singable hook.

The original concept is a humorous love song addressed to a crispy kitchen appliance / air fryer: the hum, kitchen counter, warm food, golden-brown results, buttons, midnight snacks and friends who do not understand the attachment. Keep the comic premise clear without making the lyrics sound like an advert or product endorsement.

## WhityRay adaptation rules

- Singer-first melodic delivery; no long rap verse.
- Keep the playful romantic premise, but write fresh original lines and melodies.
- Use natural Nigerian English/Pidgin lightly; don't force Ijaw words into every line.
- Add only tasteful Ijaw Wave identity where it feels natural: a small river-side / midnight-kitchen image, a warm home feeling, or a subtle River Son ad-lib.
- Use conversational phrases people might actually say.
- Keep the hook short, repetitive, charming and easy to sing after one listen.
- Let humor and affection coexist; the singer knows the situation is funny.
- No borrowed artist catchphrases, copied melodies, or imitation of another artist.
- Use only WhityRay's authorized stored voice profile in music-generation prompts. Never request a substitute singer, duet, or additional lead voice.
