# Ijaw Mouth Oral Archive — Consent & Preservation Protocol

## Purpose

Preserve everyday speech, stories, vocabulary, songs and cultural knowledge with respect for the people and communities who share them.

## Before recording

1. Explain who is collecting the material and why.
2. Ask for clear, informed permission before recording.
3. Explain where the recording and transcript may be stored and how they may be used.
4. Let the speaker choose attribution: named, community-only, pseudonymous or anonymous.
5. Ask separately before using a recording for voice cloning, synthetic speech, model training or commercial music.
6. Do not record sacred, restricted, private or ceremonial material without explicit authorization from the appropriate people.
7. Allow the speaker to decline any question or stop at any time.

## Required metadata

- recording ID;
- date and broad location (only with consent);
- speaker-selected community and language/variety;
- recording language(s);
- topic and conversational context;
- consent scope;
- attribution preference;
- transcript status;
- translator/reviewer;
- access restrictions;
- permitted uses and expiry/review date if applicable.

## Transcription

Keep the original recording and a faithful transcript separate from any normalized spelling. Mark uncertain words with timestamps. Do not silently “correct” grammar or dialect. Store translations as separate fields and identify who supplied them.

## Use restrictions

Permission to archive a recording is not permission to clone the speaker's voice, publish their story, use their words in a commercial song or train a model. Obtain separate permission for each materially different use.

## Withdrawal and correction

Provide a way for contributors to request correction, restricted access or removal from future use. Keep a record of the request and the action taken.

## Cultural review

Ask community members or appropriate knowledge holders to review sensitive cultural material. Do not present one contributor as speaking for every Ijaw community.
